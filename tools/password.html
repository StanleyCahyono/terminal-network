Add an account or change a password

Fill in the form to make a line for accounts.js. Everything happens in this browser: the password is turned into a salted hash and is never sent or saved anywhere.

Lower case; letters, numbers, dots and dashes.
Also recorded on acknowledgements and releases.
At least 10 characters.
